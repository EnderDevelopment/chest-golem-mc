# Chest Golem Plugin

The Chest Golem Plugin is a Minecraft plugin that adds a new entity called the Chest Golem. This plugin allows players to create, tame, and interact with Chest Golems, which have unique behaviors and abilities. The Chest Golem is created by placing a specific structure of stone pillars and a chest on top, and it can be tamed using Rotten Flesh. Once tamed, the Chest Golem can be used to kill players and collect their items, and it can also defend its owner by killing entities that attack them.

## Install

1. Download the plugin JAR file from the [releases page](https://github.com/EnderDevelopment/ChestGolemPlugin/releases).
2. Place the JAR file in the `plugins` folder of your Minecraft server.
3. Restart or reload your Minecraft server.

## Use

1. To create a Chest Golem, build the following structure:
   - Four stone pillars, each 2 blocks high, placed at the corners of a 3x3 area.
   - A flat roof of stone blocks covering the top of the pillars.
   - A chest with an iron trapdoor on top of the structure.
2. To tame a Chest Golem, right-click the chest with 3 Rotten Flesh in your hand.
3. Once tamed, the Chest Golem will follow you and kill players when you place it in the world. The items from the killed players will be stored in the Chest Golem's chest.
4. When the Chest Golem's chest is full, it will emerge from the ground and follow you until you open the chest to retrieve the items.
5. The Chest Golem will also kill zombies, skeletons, and creepers while following you, and it will defend its owner by killing entities that attack them when the owner has less than 2 hearts.
